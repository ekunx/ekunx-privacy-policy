EKUNX Privacy Policy

Connect. Discover. Participate. Grow.

Effective Date: October 3, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to EKUNX. EKUNX is a social community platform designed to help people connect, discover content, participate in challenges, share posts, follow other users, and interact with the community.

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, stored, and protected when you use EKUNX.

2. Information We May Collect

Depending on the features you use, EKUNX may process information such as:

  • Email address used to create and access your account.
  • Username and display name.
  • Profile picture and profile information that you choose to provide.
  • Posts, comments, likes, challenge submissions, and other content you submit.
  • Following and follower relationships.
  • Saved content and challenge participation.
  • XP, levels, rankings, streaks, and related activity.
  • Notifications and interactions with other users.
  • Technical information required to operate, secure, and troubleshoot the service.

3. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Create and manage your EKUNX account.
  • Provide profiles and social networking features.
  • Display posts, comments, likes, and other community activity.
  • Enable challenges and participation features.
  • Calculate XP, levels, rankings, and progress.
  • Send notifications related to your account and activity.
  • Protect the platform from abuse and unauthorized activity.
  • Maintain, troubleshoot, and improve EKUNX.

4. Information You Choose to Share

Information you voluntarily add to your profile or publish through EKUNX may be visible to other users depending on the relevant feature and its settings.

Please do not publish passwords, financial information, security codes, or other sensitive personal information in public posts, comments, or profile fields.

5. Third-Party Services

EKUNX may use third-party technology providers to operate parts of the service.

These services may include backend, authentication, database, hosting, security, analytics, or delivery services.

EKUNX currently uses Supabase for backend and authentication functionality and may use Cloudflare for hosting and delivery.

Third-party providers may process information as necessary to provide their services and according to their own applicable policies and terms.

6. Data Security

We aim to use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, loss, misuse, or disclosure.

However, no internet-based service can guarantee absolute security. Users should also protect their account credentials and notify EKUNX if they believe their account has been compromised.

7. Data Retention

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to operate EKUNX, provide requested services, maintain security, resolve disputes, and comply with applicable legal obligations.

8. Account Deletion

Users may request deletion of their EKUNX account and associated personal information through the account-deletion process provided by EKUNX.

Some information may need to be retained where required by law, necessary for security, or otherwise permitted by applicable law.

9. Children's Privacy

EKUNX's minimum age requirements will be stated in the application and applicable store listings.

EKUNX does not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children in violation of applicable privacy laws.

10. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live and the laws that apply to you, you may have rights concerning your personal information, including rights to request access, correction, or deletion.

You may contact EKUNX using the privacy contact information below for privacy-related questions or requests.

11. International Data Processing

EKUNX and its service providers may process or store information in countries other than the country where you live.

Where required by applicable law, appropriate safeguards should be used for international transfers of personal information.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time as EKUNX develops or as legal and operational requirements change.

The latest version will be published on this page together with its effective date.

13. Contact EKUNX

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, your personal information, or account deletion, please contact EKUNX.
