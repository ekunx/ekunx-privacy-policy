EKUNX Privacy Policy

Connect. Discover. Participate. Grow.

Effective date: October 7, 2026

1. About EKUNX

EKUNX is a social community platform for discovering content, sharing posts, following users, participating in challenges, and interacting with other people.

2. Information We Handle

Depending on your use of EKUNX, information may include your email address, username, profile details, profile picture, posts, comments, likes, follows, challenge submissions, XP, rankings, saved content, and notifications. Technical information may also be processed to operate and secure the service.

3. How Information Is Used

Information is used to manage accounts, display profiles and content, provide social features and challenges, calculate progress and rankings, deliver notifications, protect the service, and troubleshoot problems.

4. Public Content

Content and profile details may be visible to other users depending on the app's features. Please avoid publishing passwords, financial information, or sensitive personal details in posts or comments.

5. Service Providers

EKUNX uses Supabase for backend and authentication services and may use Cloudflare for hosting or delivery. Providers may process information as needed to provide their services. Actual integrations and data practices should be reflected accurately in this policy.

6. Security

Reasonable safeguards are used to help protect information from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. However, no online service can guarantee absolute security.

7. Retention and Account Deletion

Information may be kept for as long as necessary for service operation, legal obligations, dispute resolution, and security. Users may request account deletion through the available EKUNX account or support process.

8. Children's Privacy

EKUNX's age requirements and safeguards must be followed according to applicable law. EKUNX does not knowingly collect children's information in violation of applicable law.

9. Privacy Choices

Depending on applicable law, users may request access to, correction of, or deletion of their personal information. Privacy requests can be submitted using the EKUNX contact information provided below.

10. International Processing

EKUNX and its service providers may process or store information in countries other than the user's country. Applicable safeguards will be used where required by law.

11. Policy Updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated as EKUNX develops. The current version and effective date will be published on this page.

12. Contact EKUNX

Privacy contact: Please use the official EKUNX contact email provided in the app or on the official EKUNX website.
