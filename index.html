EKUNX Privacy Policy

Connect. Discover. Participate. Grow.

Effective date: October 2, 2026

1. About EKUNX

EKUNX is a social community platform for discovering content, sharing posts, following users, participating in challenges, and interacting with other people.

2. Information We Handle

Depending on your use of EKUNX, information may include your email address, username, profile details, profile picture, posts, comments, likes, follows, challenge submissions, XP, rankings, saved content, and notifications. Technical information may also be processed to operate and secure the service.

3. How Information Is Used

Information is used to manage accounts, display profiles and content, provide social features and challenges, calculate progress and rankings, deliver notifications, protect the service, and troubleshoot problems.

4. Public Content

Content and profile details may be visible to other users depending on the app's features. Please avoid publishing passwords, financial information, or sensitive personal details in posts or comments.

5. Service Providers

EKUNX uses Supabase for backend and authentication services and may use Cloudflare for hosting or delivery. Providers may process information as needed to provide their services. Actual integrations and data practices must be reflected accurately in this policy.

6. Security

7. Retention and Account Deletion

Information should be kept only as long as needed for service operation, legal obligations, dispute resolution, and security. Users should be provided with a working process to request account deletion. Deletion procedures must be verified before this policy is finalized.

8. Children's Privacy

EKUNX's age requirements and safeguards must be confirmed before launch. EKUNX should not knowingly collect children's information in violation of applicable law.

9. Privacy Choices

10. International Processing
